Getting Started

How do I create an account?
Tap Get Started in the top navigation, then choose Create Account inside the login panel. Fill in your name, email, mobile number, and address, then agree to the Privacy Policy to submit.
Why does my account need verification?
Verification confirms you are a resident of Victoria before you can file requests. An administrator reviews your details and proof of residency, which usually takes one to three working days.
Is OneVictoria free to use?
Yes. Creating an account and browsing announcements is free. Certain documents and permits may carry the standard municipal fees, which are shown before you confirm a request.

Account & Login

I forgot my password. What should I do?
On the login panel, tap Forgot password? and enter your registered email address. A reset link will be sent to that inbox. If it does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder.
The Get Started button is not responding.
This is usually a connection problem that stopped part of the page from loading. Pull down to refresh. If you opened the link from Messenger or Facebook, tap the menu icon and choose Open in browser.
How do I update my profile or address?
Log in, open your profile from the dashboard, and edit your details. Changes to your registered address may require re-verification before service requests are accepted.

Requests & Services

How do I track a request I submitted?
Open your dashboard and go to your requests list. Each entry shows its current status — pending, in review, ready, or completed — along with the date it was last updated.
How long does a document request take?
Processing time depends on the document. Most barangay certificates are ready within one to three working days, while permits requiring inspection may take longer.
Where do announcements come from?
Announcements are posted by authorized municipal staff and appear newest first on the home feed and in the announcement center.
