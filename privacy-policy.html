Privacy Policy

How the Municipality of Victoria collects, uses, and protects your personal information on OneVictoria.

Last updated: 14 August 2026

Overview

The Municipality of Victoria operates OneVictoria, a resident engagement platform. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We follow the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Information We Collect

We collect only what is needed to verify residency and deliver services:

  • Account details — full name, email address, mobile number, and password.
  • Residency details — household address, block and lot, and proof of residency documents.
  • Service records — requests, complaints, event registrations, and feedback you submit.
  • Technical data — device type, browser, and log data used to keep the service secure.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for legitimate municipal purposes:

  • Verifying that you are a resident of Victoria before granting account access.
  • Processing document requests, permits, and service transactions.
  • Sending announcements, advisories, and emergency notices relevant to your barangay.
  • Improving the reliability and accessibility of the platform.

Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Data may be shared only with the relevant municipal offices handling your request, or when disclosure is required by law, court order, or a lawful request from a government authority.

Data Security

Accounts are protected with encrypted authentication, and information is transmitted over secure connections. Access is limited to authorized personnel. While no online system can be guaranteed completely secure, we review our safeguards regularly.

Data Retention

Personal information is retained while your account is active and for as long as required by records-retention rules. You may request deletion at any time, subject to legal obligations that require us to keep certain transaction records.

Your Rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your data is processed.
  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Object to processing or withdraw consent.
  • Request erasure or blocking of your data.
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.
