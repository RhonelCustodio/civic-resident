About the platform

What is OneVictoria?

OneVictoria is the official Resident Hub of the Municipality of Victoria, Tarlac — a single online gateway that connects every resident to their local government. No more long lines or missed announcements: everything your community offers is now in one place, accessible anytime, on any device.

Create a free resident account to register for community events, volunteer your skills, track your service hours, and support municipal initiatives through secure monetary or item donations — all verified by the Municipal Office.
